Replace this with your own account of what you do. Two or three short paragraphs is plenty — enough for a reader to understand the shape of your practice before they start browsing entries.

Say who the record is for. An assessor, a prospective client and a curious colleague all want different things from the same page, and naming your reader here makes the rest of it easier to write.

Say how it's kept: what counts as an entry, how often you add to it, and what you deliberately leave out. Anything involving other people should be anonymised before it goes on a public page.
